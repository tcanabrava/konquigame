Basic Premisse:

Type: 2D platformer
Progression Type: Level based, each level unblocks a new power, that konqui can use to help him explore previous levels, or advance to the next one.
The Powers are unlockable throguh saving his friends, that are in more problems than usual.

Initial State: 
  - Konqui starts without powers, but a small fire-breath (that he can use to make light to darker areas)
  - His jump height is limited
  - His speed is limited
  - Konqui can use his cellphone (or something else) to manage his powers and equipment

Unlocable Items:
  - Jetpack: Allows konqui to jump higher than usual / float in the air for a brief period
  - Swimsuit: Allows konqui to swim on the ocean of data (to explore the software world)
  - IP Screwdriver: Allows konqui to fix small hardware issues, such as configuring the IP Address
  - TaaS: Taxi as a Service, allows konqui to fast travel between levels

  Unlockable Powers:
  - Kiki (the Krita Mascott) will teach konqui how to understand light, 
    - Activate Color blending mode, seeing things that are only visible to artists.
    - Color change: A Door is locked because the ligth is red? paint it green! Opens.
  - Kate the Woodpecker (Kate's Mascott):
    - Comment out! - throws a /* comment */ around an enemy, making it temporarely not real.
    - Infinite Loop! - Tries to hack an enemy's AI by injecting an infinite loop into their code, causing it to malfunction.
  - Kandalf: 
    - The Command Line:  literal command-line terminal window at his feet doing *some* damage to enemies
    - The Save Icon: He summons spinning, physical 3.5-inch floppy disks to orbit him, blocking incoming virus projectiles.

  (maybe more? It feels enough currently.)


The Story:
The game happens inside of a desktop computer, in a mixture of real and software world.
Konqui lives inside of a desktop computer, working to make sure everything there works correctly.
He is used to fixing smaller problems all the time, and it's ok with it. Life is boring, life is good.

The light is out, there's an explosion sound. (player starts controlling). As soon as you are able to solve the puzzle
and leave the Office, you see the computer in chaos. (Each of those points are a separate level.): 

Kandalf appears and talks to you. He is old, and has a strange sense of humor. He resents being forgotten,
but he knows you're there, and he will never give up to help you save the computer. He tells you the tale
of the sacred Quantum Processor, and how it was once a powerful computing device, but now it's just a pile of dust.
(in the end the quantum processor will be just a cat in a box.)

He tells you that your friends, and family, are trapped inside of broken pieces of the computer, and he needs you
to help save them. He can't help, you know, because of some mumble jumble. He will appear sometimes to help, and
disappear when you need giving some excuse like "I'm busy right now, come back later." or "This seems to be a glitch, try again later."

You win the game when you have saved all your friends and family, and discovers that the Quantum Processor is just a cat in a box.

Levels:

- The Qunatum Processor is inside of a box, not knowing if it's dead of alive
- The Firewall is catching fire
- The CPU and the GPU had a fight and are not in love anymore
- The Memory is behaving like an old person with dementia, forgetting everything.
- The Hard Disk is full of bad blocks that are a bad influence on the kids
- The water cooler is leaking, and not cool anymore.
- The Motherboard is being replaced by a Stepmotherboard
- The Desktop is full of dust-monsters
(or something like that. I want it to be silly, but also kinda-correct)

Enemies:
- Computer Viruses
  - Programs with viruses
- Zombie Programs
- Heat (in the form of fire-entities, they burn)
- Water (in the form of water-entities, they rust metal, and dissolve into the ground)
- The fire-water (They appear if heat and water combine, and they can make the screen distorted)
- Bad Blocks (in the form of 80's break-dancer 8 bit style blocks)
- Dust Bunnie
- The Overclocked - when the heat is up, they appear running fast through the screen, overclocking the CPU and GPU

- Phishing Hooks - Literal hooks from the ceiling with items. 
- Bloatware - In the form of heavy enemies
